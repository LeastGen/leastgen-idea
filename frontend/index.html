Two modes, one goal: accelerate research

Whether you need a quick novelty check or a full research proposal, IdeaFlow adapts to your workflow.

🔍

Scoop-Check

7-step novelty verification in 2-5 minutes. Enter a problem + claimed novelty, get a 5-level verdict with prior-art hits from arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex.

⚡

IdeaSpark Pipeline

12-phase pipeline from a research direction to a complete idea card with methodology, equations, literature grounding, and falsification predictions. 20-40 minutes end-to-end.

📚

Multi-Source Literature

Searches arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, and OpenReview. Full-text PDF fetching and caching for deep analysis of relevant papers.

📐

KaTeX Math Rendering

Equations in the final idea card render beautifully with KaTeX. Export to PDF, DOCX, or Markdown for journal submission.

🌍

Field-Agnostic

Works for any discipline: biology, linguistics, sociology, materials science, education, policy, medicine, economics — any research field.

🔒

Self-Hosted & Private

Your data stays on your server. Open source (MIT), no vendor lock-in. Deploy on your own infrastructure with one command.
