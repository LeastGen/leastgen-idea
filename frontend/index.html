Two modes. One corpus.

Novelty check or full proposal. Same sources.

Scoop-Check

7-step novelty verification in 2-5 minutes. Enter a problem + claimed novelty, get a 5-level verdict with prior-art hits from arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex.

IdeaSpark Pipeline

12-phase pipeline from a research direction to a complete idea card with methodology, equations, literature grounding, and concrete ways to test it. 20-40 minutes end-to-end.

Multi-Source Literature

Searches arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, and OpenReview. Full-text PDF fetching and caching for deep analysis of relevant papers.

KaTeX Math Rendering

Equations in the final idea card render with KaTeX. Export to PDF, DOCX, or Markdown.

Field-Agnostic

Any discipline: biology, linguistics, sociology, materials science, education, policy, medicine, economics.

Self-Hosted

Data stays on your server. MIT licensed. Deploy on your infrastructure with one command.
